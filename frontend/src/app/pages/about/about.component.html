Our story

Daataa Direct Limited is based in Rawalpindi. We take on the parts of a business that need a steady team: software systems, graphic design, legal and trademark, shipping and logistics, accounts, and day-to-day operations.

Hours are 11am to 8pm, Pakistan time. We reply within one working day.

Fields we cover

Operations covers production, administration, procurement, shipping and logistics, human resources, accounts and tax, and legal and trademark.

Commerce covers marketing, sales, customer service, graphic design and e-commerce, including listings and new products.

Technology covers software systems, IT backup and troubleshooting, research and development, and quality assurance.

Why teams work with us

  • Software, legal, logistics and graphic design in one company
  • Operations, commerce and technology handled together
  • Remote delivery from the Rawalpindi team
  • A reply within one working day
